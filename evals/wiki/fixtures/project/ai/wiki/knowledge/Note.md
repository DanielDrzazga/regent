# Note

A [[User]]'s record: a title and a body, with a creation date.

- the title is trimmed of whitespace at the start and end; rules: [[A note must have a title]],
  [[Title length limit]];
- the body may be empty;
- order on the list: [[Notes are listed newest first]];
- a Note is never deleted: [[Archiving instead of deleting]].
